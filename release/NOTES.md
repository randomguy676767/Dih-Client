## Dih Client 1.15.0 for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric)

**Install:** download `dih-client-1.15.0+mc1.21.11.jar` below and put it in your `mods` folder (needs Fabric Loader + Fabric API). Keep only one Dih jar there.
Already on 1.15.0 or newer? Dih updates itself from this page — just restart Minecraft when it says an update is ready.

### New
- **Auto-updates** from GitHub Releases (Mods tab: Auto-update, Check now). Old jars move to `dih-old-versions`, nothing is deleted
- Extra Dih copies in the mods folder are tidied up on start
- **Your own menu backgrounds** in `config/dih-backgrounds` (`menu_background.png`, `menu_background_christmas.png`), kept on every update
- **Voice word counter**: say your word out loud and Dih counts it (offline speech recognition with Vosk; mic recorded through OpenAL like Simple Voice Chat)
- VR with Vivecraft (F7), Essential support, Spotify lyrics with album art
